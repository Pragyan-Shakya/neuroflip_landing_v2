## The idea

Review a topic just as you're about to forget it. Each review pushes the next one further out.

## A starting schedule

- Day 1: learn it.
- Day 3: first review.
- Day 7: second review.
- Day 21: third review.

Keep sessions short and focused. Consistency beats marathon days.
