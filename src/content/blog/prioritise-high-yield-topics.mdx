## Why priority matters

With more than a thousand topics across 19 subjects, you can't give everything equal time. Past questions show which topics come up again and again.

## A simple three-step approach

1. Start with the topics that past papers test most often.
2. Revise them until you can answer confidently.
3. Move down the list, milestone by milestone.

> Past questions set revision priority. They don't predict the next paper.

### What to avoid

- Spending weeks on rarely tested topics early on.
- Skipping full-syllabus coverage entirely.
